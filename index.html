BEHIND THE CHAT

How it
works.

A small, transparent agent loop. No hidden CRM or stored lead profile.

01

Open & qualify

Mila starts from the commented post. The agent learns about running level, goal, deadline, blocker, weekly time, and readiness through a natural back-and-forth.

02

Handle & decide

It responds to price, time, or hesitation without pressure. A ready, qualified lead gets two real-looking demo slots. Someone not ready gets a free run-week planner.

03

Structure & protect

Every model turn requests JSON. The app validates fields and stage transitions, rejects unsupported bookings, redacts contact-like data, and falls back safely if output is invalid.
